## 👋 About Me

I'm currently transitioning into Software Quality Assurance while studying **Multiplatform Application Development (DAM)**.

Before pursuing QA, I gained several years of experience in quality-focused operations at **Amazon**, where I validated product data, investigated inconsistencies, and worked with detailed internal processes. That experience strengthened my analytical thinking, attention to detail, and structured approach to quality—skills that I now apply to software testing.

I enjoy understanding how software works, exploring business workflows, and identifying opportunities to improve software quality throughout the development lifecycle. This portfolio reflects my hands-on learning through practical testing, structured QA documentation, and real-world projects.

My long-term goal is to grow into a Software QA Engineer with expertise in both Manual Testing and Test Automation.
